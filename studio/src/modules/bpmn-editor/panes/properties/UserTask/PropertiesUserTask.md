# User Task

![User Task](UserTask.svg)

A `User Task` is a step that a person completes. The process pauses at the task and places it on the assigned people's task lists. When someone opens it, they see a form, fill it in, and finish it — and the process continues with whatever they entered.

## Who can work on it

- **Assignees** — the people or groups allowed to see, reserve, and finish the task. See [Assignees](help://bpmn/properties/user_task_assignees).

## What the person sees and does

- **Form Fields** — the form shown to the person. Design it in the Form Builder; whatever they enter becomes part of the process data when they finish.
- **Due Date** — when the task should be completed by. It can be a fixed date or worked out from the process data.
- **Priority** — a number you can use to sort and highlight more urgent tasks.

## Data Pipeline

Like all activities, a User Task can shape the data it works with. See [Input Mappings](help://bpmn/properties/input_mappings), [Output Mappings](help://bpmn/properties/output_mappings), [Payload Contract](help://bpmn/properties/payload_contract), and [Result Contract](help://bpmn/properties/result_contract).
