# Group

![Group](Group.svg)

The `Group` is an Artifact that provides the functionality to visualize related `Groups` of `Activities`, `Events` or any other part of a created BPMN diagram.
The `Group` has no restrictions of `Participants`, `Pools` or `Process`.
As an `Artifact`, a `Group` cannot have a connection with `Sequence Flows` or `Message Flows`.
